# Lesson Plans & Teaching Guides

### Overview

As a member of the Instructional Staff, keeping your classroom educational, engaging, and age-appropriate is your central focus. This guide provides the structure required to manage your classroom and deliver lessons across all official subjects.

### Classroom Structure Guide

**1. Opening the Class**
* **Arrival & Attendance:** Arrive at your designated classroom on time. Greet students as they enter and instruct them to take a seat.
* **Topic Introduction:** Clearly announce the subject and specific topic of the day so students know what to focus on.

**2. Delivering the Lesson**
* **Instructional Focus:** Keep the lesson centered entirely on the designated subject. Use interactive questions to keep students engaged.
* **Classroom Order:** Maintain a professional, respectful environment. Ensure students do not talk over the lesson or disrupt their peers.

**3. Dismissal**
* **Wrap-Up:** Briefly review the key points of the lesson or allow students to ask final questions.
* **Official Dismissal:** Formally dismiss the students from the classroom once the session is concluded, instructing them to move orderly to their next area.

### Subject Curriculums

#### Core Subjects

**Science:** Cover fundamental earth, life, or physical science concepts through structured classroom discussions and step-by-step virtual lab demonstrations.

**English:** Focus on reading comprehension, creative story analysis, writing prompts, and structural grammar exercises.

**History:** Teach historical eras, world civilizations, and major global events using interactive timelines and discussions.

#### Elective Subjects

**Cooking:** Guide students through recipe reading, kitchen safety rules, and the step-by-step creation of various culinary dishes.

**Drama:** Host script-reading sessions, improvisational acting exercises, and short stage performances to build student confidence.

**Gym:** Lead organized physical activities, team sports, and fitness routines while emphasizing teamwork and sportsmanship.

**Art:** Instruct students on artistic techniques, color theory, and creative design projects using the classroom workspace.
