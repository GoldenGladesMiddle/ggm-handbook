# Session Hosting Guide

Welcome to the official Golden Glades Middle Session Hosting Guide. As a host, you are the backbone of our school's operation. Please follow these procedures to ensure a professional and enjoyable experience for all students and staff.

## I. Pre-Session Checklist
Before starting, ensure all requirements are met to prevent mid-session issues.

* **User Authorization**: Confirm you have hosting permissions. Contact the Director or Vice Director if you are unsure.
* **Server Verification**: Check that the automated school systems (bells, systems) are active. Notify a Developer immediately if any systems are unresponsive.
* **Staff Check-In**: Verify you have enough staff and instructors. Ensure they are prepared to cover our core subjects: Science, Cooking, English, Drama, Gym, History, and Art.
* **Class Assignments**: Assign Subs and Teaching Aides to classrooms and ensure Hall Monitors are stationed at high-traffic areas.
* **Announcements**: Post your announcements to the Group Announcements using the templates below.

**Session Ending:**
* **Title:** `SESSION ENDING`
* **Content:** `The school session is now officially over! Thank you to everyone who joined us for a day of learning and fun. We hope you enjoyed the classes today. Staff, thank you for cooperating with us to make this session a success.`

## III. In-Game Server Messages (:sm)
Use these commands to keep the student body informed throughout the day.

* **Session Start**: `:sm [SESSION] 🔔 A new session has begun. Students, head on down to the cafeteria to have a delicious breakfast!`
* **First Bell**: `:sm [FIRST BELL] 🔔 Class is now in session! Students, please find your way to your designated class located at the top of your screen. Late arrivals will need a pass from the main office!`
* **Transition**: `:sm [TRANSITION] 🔔 The bell has rung for Period [Number]! All students, please report to your designated classrooms. Staff will begin hall sweeps soon! 🏫`
* **Dismissal**: `:sm [DISMISSAL] 🔔 Thank you for coming to Golden Glades Middle. Don't forget to join our Roblox group for updates! ✨`

## V. Administrative Commands
Administrators must use these tools responsibly to maintain order.

* `:sm [message]` – Important school-wide intercom alerts.
* `:info [username]` – Verify user account age and membership type.
* `:ff [username]` – Provide ForceField for protection or spawn management.
* `:glow [username]` – Highlight player (used by Security and School Administrators during passing periods).
* `:playmusic [ID] [true/false] / :stopmusic` – Used during passing periods, lunch, and breaks. At the end of the command, type true or false for the loop setting. (Set to true by default.)
