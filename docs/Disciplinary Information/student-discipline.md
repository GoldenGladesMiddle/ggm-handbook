# Student Disciplinary & General Principles

### Overview of Student Conduct
Students are expected to adhere to all School Rules and treat staff with respect. The school employs a progressive discipline system to ensure consequences are commensurate with behavior. Serious misconduct may lead to a suspension, kick, or ban, and it is required that all punishments are both fair and clearly explained to the student.

### Offense Categories & Actions

#### Minor Offenses
*Examples include spamming chat or being disruptive, refusing to follow simple instructions, or wandering out of class without permission.*
* **1st offense:** Verbal Warning.
* **2nd offense:** Written Warning via the GGM Assistant bot.
* **3rd offense:** Temporary removal from class/session or server ban if needed.

#### Moderate Offenses
*Examples include repeated disruption after multiple warnings, skipping classes/events without reason, or disrespecting staff or other students.*
* **1st offense:** Written Warning + Detention.
* **2nd offense:** In School Suspension (One Day).
* **3rd offense:** Out School Suspension (Two Days).

#### Serious Offenses
*Examples include bullying, harassment, or targeted trolling, spamming or flooding chat to disrupt gameplay, repeated refusal to follow any staff instructions, or using admin/exploit powers if somehow gained access.*
* **Actions:** Immediate suspension pending review, possible long-term suspension (1-2 weeks), or a possible permanent ban from the school game/group.

#### Extreme Offenses (Zero Tolerance)
*Examples include exploiting or hacking in game, posting inappropriate/offensive content, harassment involving threats/unsafe behavior/TOS violations, or attempting to sabotage the school.*
* **Actions:** Immediate ban from game/server, permanent removal from the school group/game, and reporting to Roblox and Discord if necessary.

### Appeals Process
* Students may appeal suspensions or bans by submitting a ticket in Discord under the #support channel.
* Appeals must be respectful and honest.
* The final decision rests with administration.
