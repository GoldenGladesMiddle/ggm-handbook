# Rank Descriptions Guide

### School Security, Office Secretary, Nurse, Guidance Counselor, and Teaching Aides

* Staff members responsible for supporting daily campus operations.
* Responsibilities include maintaining safety, managing their respective stations, assisting primary educators, and addressing student needs.
* To be promoted, staff members must be noticed by a Vice Director or Director inside or outside of a school session.

### Substitute Teacher

* Staff members who have been recognized by a Vice Director or Director.
* Responsibilities include maintaining classroom order, managing student behavior, and ensuring smooth school operations.
* Granted access to moderator commands and are expected to hide their commands and use them responsibly.
* To be promoted, staff members must be recognized by a Vice Director or Director inside or outside of a school session.

### Core Teacher

* Staff members who have been recognized by a Vice Director or Director.
* Responsibilities include leading classroom lessons, keeping instruction educational and appropriate, and managing student behavior.
* Granted access to moderator commands and are expected to hide their commands and use them responsibly.
* To be promoted, staff members must be recognized by a Vice Director or Director inside or outside of a school session.

### Elective Teacher

* Staff members who have been recognized by a Vice Director or Director.
* Responsibilities include leading specialized subject lessons, maintaining classroom order, and assisting students.
* Granted access to moderator commands and are expected to hide their commands and use them responsibly.
* To be promoted, staff members must be recognized by a Vice Director or Director inside or outside of a school session.

### School Administrator

* Staff members who have been recognized by a Vice Director or Director.
* Responsible for various high-level tasks, including supervising school operations to ensure smooth functioning, handling student discipline, banning rule violators on campus, and hosting school sessions.
* Granted access to admin commands and are expected to hide their commands and use them responsibly.
* This is the highest attainable rank, this rank indicates that a staff member has shown exemplary behavior in order to receive this promotion.

### Vice Director

* Staff members responsible for executive-level management, including supervising operations, approving suspensions or permanent bans, reviewing account recovery and rank transfer requests, hosting and ranking at sessions, and conducting official investigations.
* This rank is not attainable through standard promotions.

### Director

* Staff members responsible for the oversight of Golden Glades Middle School, including establishing policies, overseeing investigations, determining final promotion and demotion actions, and managing the leadership team.
* This rank is not attainable through standard promotions.
