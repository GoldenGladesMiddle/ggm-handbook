# Shift & Inactivity (LOA) Policy

### Overview

To maintain smooth operations at Golden Glades Middle, all staff members are expected to remain active and meet weekly shift and availability requirements. When real-life commitments interfere with staff duties, an absence or Leave of Absence (LOA) must be logged.

### Weekly Shift & Availability Requirements

- **Instructional Staff & General Staff:** Active on at least three weekdays (Monday–Friday).
- **Guidance Counselors:** Active on at least three weekdays (Monday–Friday).
- **Administrators:** Consistent weekday availability (Monday–Friday).
- **Logging:** All shift activity must be logged using official logging procedures or automated Discord channels.

### Leave of Absence (LOA) & Absence Guidelines

#### Overview & Responsibilities
- The forum serves as a dedicated space for staff members to communicate absences, request leaves of absence, and keep management informed of staff availability.
- **Lesson Plans:** Core Teachers and Elective Teachers must upload their class lesson plan when submitting an absence or LOA request.

#### Eligibility & Duration
- **Maximum Duration:** Standard LOAs may last up to **14 consecutive days**.
- **Extensions:** Requests for extensions beyond 14 days require approval from the **School Director** or Senior Administration.
- **Frequency:** A minimum of 14 active days must pass between consecutive LOA requests under normal circumstances.

#### Types of Inactivity Requests
1. **Full LOA:** Complete absence from all staff duties, game server activity, and Discord responsibilities.
2. **Partial Inactivity:** Reduced activity where minimal duties (e.g., occasional ticket responses or administrative logging) are still performed.

### How to Post an Absence or LOA
When submitting a post in the designated forum, **always make the title of the post your name and the date submitted**.

### Unexcused Inactivity & Consequences

Failing to meet shift or availability quotas without an approved absence or LOA will result in disciplinary action:

- **1st Week (Unexcused):** Official Warning.
- **2nd Consecutive Week:** Demotion or suspension.
- **3rd Consecutive Week:** Termination from the Golden Glades Middle staff team.
