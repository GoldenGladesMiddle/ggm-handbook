# Staff Disciplinary & General Principles

### Overview of Staff Conduct
* All staff are expected to follow the Staff Regulations.
* Discipline is progressive; minor infractions result in minor punishments, while repeated or severe issues lead to stronger actions.
* Serious misconduct may result in immediate demotion or removal.
* All actions should be documented in staff-warnings, which can include a staff report, screenshot, or admin log.
* If a staff member leaves the group while on suspension, they will not be able to receive their role again.

### Offense Categories & Actions

#### 1. Minor Offenses
*Examples include arriving late to class without notice, small mistakes with admin commands, ignoring instructions from administration, and slight unprofessional behavior, such as joking too much or going off-topic.*
* **1st offense:** Verbal/DM Warning.
* **2nd offense:** Written Warning (logged by admin).
* **3rd offense:** Temporary suspension (1-2 days).

#### 2. Moderate Offenses
*Examples include inactivity without notice over 4 days, using admin/mod powers for fun, and being disrespectful towards students or staff.*
* **1st offense:** Written Warning + Supervision.
* **2nd offense:** Temporary Demotion or Suspension (2-3 days).
* **3rd offense:** Demotion to Student or Permanent Removal.

#### 3. Serious Offenses
*Examples include abusing admin powers, harassing or bullying students/staff, leaking staff-only information, or intentionally ruining roleplay sessions or lessons.*
* **Actions:** Immediate suspension while administration reviews, possible demotion or permanent removal from staff, and a possible ban from the Roblox group or game if necessary.

#### 4. Extreme Offenses (Zero Tolerance)
*Examples include exploiting or hacking in game, harassment involving inappropriate/unsafe content, attempting to sabotage the school, or breaking Roblox or Discord TOS in any way.*
* **Actions:** Immediate termination of the staff position, permanent ban from the school/group, and reporting to Roblox and Discord if necessary.

### Appeals Process
* Staff may appeal disciplinary actions by submitting an appeal ticket in the Discord server.
* If permanently banned, staff must submit an appeal ticket after the one-week time ban in the Discord server.
* Appeals must be respectful and honest.
* Decisions made after the appeal process are final.
