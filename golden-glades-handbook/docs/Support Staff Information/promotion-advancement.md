# Promotion and Advancement Guide

### Daily Operations & Staff Progression

To receive a promotion at Golden Glades, staff members are expected to actively participate in standard daily school sessions and campus operations. Active sessions serve as primary opportunities for staff to be noticed for advancement. To be eligible for a promotion, it is crucial to consistently maintain professionalism, utilize proper grammar, and demonstrate clear dedication to the community.

**Grammar Expectations:** While strict grammar enforcement is not universally mandated for all entry-level staff roles, utilizing correct capitalization, punctuation, and spelling is strictly required while in-game if you wish to be considered for a promotion.

**General Promotion Policy:** Active attendance and exhibiting exemplary behavior do not guarantee an immediate promotion, as advancements are determined by a combination of performance factors. Staff members are expected to remain patient and not complain about or query their promotion status. Recommending other staff members for advancement is strictly prohibited.

**Direct Recognition:** Staff members can earn a promotion by being noticed for exceptional leadership, operational dedication, and classroom management by a Vice Director or Director during standard school hours.

### Core Evaluation Requirements

To significantly increase your chances of being promoted to senior positions, you must meet several key operational requirements. Higher promotions happen dynamically based on leadership evaluations. Fulfilling these requirements improves your eligibility but does not guarantee an immediate rank adjustment:

#### Requirement #1 — In-Game Activity & Balance

To be considered for higher advancement, candidates must spend consistent, well-distributed time actively managing the campus or assisting in classrooms each week.

**Healthy Balance:** Overworking is explicitly discouraged; Vice Directors and Directors highly value a healthy balance between Roblox and your personal life.

**Operational Focus:** While in-game, focus on high-impact duties—such as managing student behavior, leading classroom sessions, or handling administration. Your interactions should be unique, positive, and demonstrate a flawless understanding of Golden Glades' rules and procedures.

#### Requirement #2 — Platform Engagement

Staff members must maintain an active presence in the school’s Discord server to be eligible for a promotion by a Vice Director or Director.

**Community Support:** It is highly recommended that you assist other staff members with their questions and provide accurate information.

**Communication:** Frequently chatting in the Discord server and actively participating in school-wide events is highly beneficial for those aiming for upper leadership positions.

#### Requirement #3 — Advanced Grammar

Candidates for higher ranks must maintain excellent grammar, proper punctuation, and mature spelling during all in-game interactions. Proper presentation demonstrates the professionalism, maturity, and role-model behavior expected of school leaders. Accumulating frequent or careless grammar errors will directly reduce your chances of promotion.

#### Requirement #4 — Professional Maturity

Maturity is a crucial factor when making promotion decisions. Immature conversations, erratic behavior, or publicly arguing with students will severely hurt your advancement opportunities. Constantly discussing your rank or lobbying for promotions is viewed as a lack of professionalism; leadership looks for well-rounded staff who are genuinely focused on serving the student community.

#### Requirement #5 — Strict Anti-Hinting Policy

Complaining about the promotion system or hinting for rank advancements may lead directly to disciplinary actions, warnings, or demotions. Hinting suggests an overemphasis on personal rank rather than a commitment to providing an excellent educational environment for our students.

### Common Misconceptions

**Activity Monitoring:** Vice Directors and Directors monitor staff activity through various digital platforms and internal logs; they are not required to be present in-game at all times to evaluate your performance.

**Recommendations:** Certain Administrative Staff hold the official authority to recommend eligible staff for promotion based on their daily observations and leadership performance.
